### Docs

- Proposal 0178 (OS capabilities for tooling) moved to
  `docs/proposals/implemented/`. All six stages shipped: `Flow.Path`, recoverable
  `IoError` + `Result`-returning I/O, `Flow.Fs`, `Flow.Crypto`, `Flow.Env` with
  argv plumbing, and `Flow.Process`.

  Its four surviving questions did not move with it — they are tracked as KI-004
  through KI-007 in `docs/known_issues.md`, where they stay visible: native
  subprocess execution being POSIX-only, `Flow.Fs` not being async-aware, `String`
  rather than `Bytes` as the I/O currency, and the lack of streaming reads.
